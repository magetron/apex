# Car models

The `.glb` car bodies in this folder are adapted from models by
Ddiaz Design on Sketchfab, licensed
[CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).

Changes: converted to glTF, aligned to each car's wheelbase, wheels separated, engine bays
removed, compressed.

These adapted models are shared under the same licence (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0): non-commercial use,
with attribution, and adaptations under the same terms. The per-model titles are listed in
`src/vehicle3d/gltf/cars/` and on the site's About page.
